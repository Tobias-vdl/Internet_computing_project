Dragon's Lair

Running the Game

  1. Install the packages: npm install
  2. Start the server: node gameServer.js
  3. Open http://localhost:8000 in two (or more) browser windows. Use a private/incognito window or a different browser for the second player, so the two players have separate login sessions.

Several pairs of players can play at the same time: every pair gets its own game room on the server.

Game Play Page

Description

Dragon's Lair is a two-player competitive dungeon game.

  • The map is a dungeon with stone walls forming corridors and chambers.
  • A dragon sits in the centre of the dungeon and occasionally spits fireballs that set tiles of the map on fire.
  • Both players try to slay the dragon, but only one of them can become the dragon-slayer and claim the honour that comes with it.
  • Players can pick up arrows for their bows, which they can use to damage the dragon or kill each other.
  • Killing the other player leaves them dead for a short while before they respawn.
  • Shooting the dragon decreases its life until it eventually dies.

Things in the Game

  • Players: two differently coloured animated knight sprites, one per player.
  • Dragon: an animated boss in the centre, with a separate HP bar for each player.
  • Fireballs
    • The dragon launches fireballs that land on random tiles.
    • A warning marker appears on the target tile shortly before impact.
    • A landed fireball burns on that tile for some time before disappearing.
    • A player who walks into a burning tile dies.
  • Arrows
    • Arrows spawn only at fixed spawn points.
    • Each player can carry a maximum of three arrows.
  • Walls: block movement and shape the dungeon.

How to Play

  • Move with WASD.
  • Attack with Space.
  • Pick up an arrow by walking onto it.
  • Each attack consumes an arrow, shooting it in the direction the player is facing.
  • An arrow hitting:
    • The dragon: damages the dragon's HP bar of the player who shot the arrow.
    • The other player: kills them.
  • Dying and respawning
    • A player who dies from a fireball or from the other player loses all their arrows.
    • The player respawns at their start position after a short while.

How to Win or Lose

  • The dragon has a separate HP bar for each player.
  • The first player to reduce their HP bar to zero slays the dragon and wins; the other player loses.
  • A normal game lasts at most 200 seconds (3 min 20 sec).
  • If the dragon has not been slain when the time runs out, nobody claims the glory and the game ends in a draw.

Game Over Page

When the dragon is slain or the time runs out, both players are shown the game over page. It contains:

  • The winner of the game, or a message declaring a draw.
  • The time elapsed.
  • Statistics for each player:
    • arrows fired
    • arrows hit
    • accuracy (hits / fired)
    • damage dealt to the dragon
    • deaths
    • kills of the other player
  • Overall ranking of the top 10 players across all games, stored on the server in data/rankings.json. Players are ranked by number of wins, then by total damage dealt to the dragon, then by their fastest win. Wins, draws, losses, dragon damage, kills, average kills per game and fastest win are shown, and the players of the game just played are highlighted.
  • Buttons
    • Play Again goes straight to a new game lobby, where the player is paired with the next player who joins (e.g. the same opponent pressing Play Again).
    • Back to Menu returns to the home page.

Cheating

  • Pressing the G key toggles "God Mode" on and off for the player pressing it.
  • In God Mode, the cheating player is invincible and has infinite arrows.
