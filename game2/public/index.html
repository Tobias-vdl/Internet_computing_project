The Dragon's Lair

Two knights. One dragon and a Golden Rubber Duck.

The Goal

Slay the dragon and claim the Golden Rubber Duck. Don't die (and maybe stop the other knight).

Deep beneath the mountain, a dragon guards the most precious treasure in the land: the legendary Golden Rubber Duck. Two knights have entered its lair, but only the one who slays the dragon gets to take the duck home - and enjoy a well-earned victory bath. Gather arrows, strike the dragon before your rival does, and watch out for the other knight who wants to steal the glory - and the duck. Torches are burning low, and the lair's ancient gates will seal at dawn. Slay the dragon before they close, or no one goes home the victor!

The dragon is also opposed to dying. It spits fireballs: a tile glows red as a warning, then bursts into flames for a few seconds. Stay off it.

The Rules

  • Each knight has their own Dragon HP bar. Only your arrows drain yours, and the first knight to empty it slays the dragon and claims the Golden Rubber Duck.
  • You can carry up to 3 arrows, and you can shoot once per second. Arrows you pick up reappear a few seconds later.
  • Arrows hurt knights too. A fallen knight returns to their starting spot after 3 seconds.
  • You have 200 seconds before the gates close. If the dragon is still alive when time runs out, nobody claims the glory and it’s a draw.

The Controls

  • Move your knight with W A S D
  • Shoot arrows with Space (if you have any)
  • Don't press G (That is cheating!)
