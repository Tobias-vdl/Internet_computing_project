DRAGON'S LAIR DUEL – MAP DESIGN

WallBlocks movement and arrows. Ghost mode (G) lets a player walk through walls.
FloorWalkable tiles. Fireballs only land on floor tiles.
Dragon's lairThe ring of tiles around the dragon, guarded by four pillars.
DragonA 3×3 boss in the centre. Each player has a separate dragon HP bar – your arrows only lower yours.
Captive princessLocked in a cage behind the dragon. The player who slays the dragon frees her and wins.
BraziersStand in front of and on both sides of the dragon. Together with the cage they block every straight shot, so players must aim from an angle.
Arrow spawnsThree spots on each side. The active side alternates between left and right. One arrow = one shot.
Player spawnsP1 (blue) starts at the top, P2 (red) at the bottom – equally far from both arrow sides.
FireballsA warning marker for 1 second, then the tile burns for about 4 seconds.
